Research

ACM CHI 2026

Mind the Gap: Mapping Wearer–Bystander Privacy Tensions and Context-Adaptive Pathways for Camera Glasses

X. Wang, K. Peng, X. Yi, H. Li

Do you know if someone is recording you? A study of privacy tensions between people wearing camera glasses and the people around them, from a survey (N=525) and paired interviews (N=20).

Paper: Mind the Gap, PDF (opens in a new tab)Video: Mind the Gap, on YouTube (opens in a new tab)

IEEE VR 2025 (selected as TVCG paper)

VR Whispering: A Multisensory Approach for Private Conversations in Social Virtual Reality

X. Wang*, K. Peng*, C. Hao, W. Yu, X. Yi, H. Li  (*equal contribution)

People care about privacy, but how do you make them feel it? Whisper combines visual, auditory, and tactile cues to recreate the feel of whispering in VR. In a comparison with existing methods (N=24), it did better on privacy, intimacy, and social presence.

Paper: VR Whispering, PDF (opens in a new tab)Video: VR Whispering, on YouTube (opens in a new tab)

UIST 2026 (SIC)

Harmonia: Physiological Signal-Driven Music for Remote Relationship Maintenance

K. Peng, N. Zhang

Messages and calls don't tell you how someone far away is really doing. Harmonia turns real-time physiological signals into ambient generative music, giving people in long-distance relationships a shared channel that evolves with the relationship.

Paper: Harmonia, PDF (opens in a new tab)Video: Harmonia, on YouTube (opens in a new tab)
